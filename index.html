Newsletter Digest — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Newsletter Digest ("the app") is a personal, single-user script that summarizes email newsletters. It is operated by an individual for their own use and is not a commercial product. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

What the app accesses

Using the Gmail API, the app reads messages in the account owner's own Gmail inbox that come from a short, hard-coded list of newsletter sender addresses. It also uses permission to send email in order to deliver the finished digest back to that same account. The app does not read, index, or store any other messages in the inbox.

How the data is used

The text of those newsletter emails is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which generates a summarized digest. The digest is then emailed back to the account owner's own inbox. The app has no other purpose and performs no other action with the data.

Third parties

  • Google (Gmail API) — to read the newsletter emails and send the digest.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — to summarize the newsletter content. Content sent to Anthropic is handled under Anthropic's own privacy and data-use terms.

The app does not sell, rent, or share data with any other party, and does not use the data for advertising or profiling.

Storage and retention

The app runs locally on the owner's own computer. A Google OAuth token is stored locally in a file named token.json so the app can run on a schedule without re-authorizing each time. Newsletter content and generated digests are not retained by the app beyond the run that produces them; the digest lives only in the owner's own email inbox. There is no central server and no database.

Limited use

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app owner at the contact email associated with this project.
